COMPANY POLICY HANDBOOK

=================================
1. WORK FROM HOME POLICY
=================================

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

Employees must:
- Be available between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time.
- Attend all mandatory meetings.
- Maintain a stable internet connection.
- Follow company security guidelines.

Remote work requests exceeding 3 days per week require HR approval.

=================================
2. ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
=================================

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Unused leave:
- Up to 5 days can be carried forward.
- Remaining days expire at year-end.

Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance.

=================================
3. INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
=================================

All employees must:
- Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Change passwords every 90 days.
- Report phishing emails immediately.
- Encrypt confidential company data.
- Lock systems when away from their workstation.

Sharing company credentials is strictly prohibited.

Violation of security policies may result in disciplinary action.

=================================
4. ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
=================================

Company devices are intended primarily for business use.

Employees must not:
- Install unauthorized software.
- Download pirated content.
- Access malicious or illegal websites.
- Share confidential information externally.

Monitoring may be performed to ensure compliance.

=================================
5. INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
=================================

Security incidents must be reported within 30 minutes of discovery.

Incident severity levels:

Critical:
- Data breach
- Ransomware infection
- Unauthorized system access

High:
- Malware infection
- Privilege escalation

Medium:
- Policy violations
- Unauthorized software installation

The Security Team will investigate and document all incidents.

=================================
6. DEVSECOPS POLICY
=================================

All code changes must:
- Pass SAST scanning.
- Pass dependency vulnerability scanning.
- Undergo peer review.
- Have no Critical or High vulnerabilities.

Container images must be scanned before deployment.

Secrets must never be hardcoded in source code.

=================================
7. APPLICATION SECURITY POLICY
=================================

Development teams must:
- Follow secure coding guidelines.
- Conduct threat modeling for critical applications.
- Perform security testing before production releases.
- Remediate Critical vulnerabilities within 24 hours.
- Remediate High vulnerabilities within 7 days.

OWASP Top 10 risks must be reviewed during development.

=================================
8. AI USAGE POLICY
=================================

Employees may use approved AI tools for productivity purposes.

Employees must not:
- Upload customer PII to public AI systems.
- Upload source code without approval.
- Share confidential business information with external AI services.

All AI-generated content must be reviewed by a human before publication.

=================================
9. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
=================================

Employees may claim:
- Travel expenses
- Hotel accommodation
- Business meals
- Conference registrations

Claims exceeding INR 50,000 require director approval.

Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

=================================
10. EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING POLICY
=================================

New employees must complete:
- Security Awareness Training
- Code of Conduct Training
- Data Privacy Training
- MFA Enrollment

Completion is required within the first 14 days of employment.

=================================
11. DATA PRIVACY POLICY
=================================

Employees must collect, process, and store personal data only for approved business purposes.

Personal data must not be shared externally unless there is a valid business need, legal basis, and approval from the Data Privacy or Legal team.

Customer PII must be masked or anonymized before being used in testing, analytics, or AI systems.

Any suspected privacy incident must be reported immediately to the Security and Privacy teams.

=================================
12. THIRD-PARTY VENDOR SECURITY POLICY
=================================

All third-party vendors handling company or customer data must complete a security review before onboarding.

Vendor assessments may include:
- Security questionnaire review
- Data protection agreement validation
- Vulnerability management review
- Compliance certification review
- Incident response capability review

High-risk vendors must be reviewed annually.

=================================
13. PASSWORD AND ACCESS MANAGEMENT POLICY
=================================

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must not be reused across personal and company accounts.

Privileged access must be approved by the system owner and reviewed every quarter.

Access must be removed within 24 hours when an employee leaves the company or changes role.

Shared accounts are not allowed unless explicitly approved for operational reasons.

=================================
14. CLOUD SECURITY POLICY
=================================

Cloud resources must follow the principle of least privilege.

Public access to storage buckets, databases, and management interfaces is prohibited unless formally approved.

Cloud workloads must have logging, monitoring, and encryption enabled.

Infrastructure-as-Code templates must be reviewed before production deployment.

=================================
15. SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT LIFECYCLE POLICY
=================================

Security activities must be integrated into every phase of the software development lifecycle.

Requirements phase:
- Identify security and privacy requirements.

Design phase:
- Perform threat modeling for sensitive or internet-facing applications.

Development phase:
- Follow secure coding practices.
- Avoid hardcoded secrets.

Testing phase:
- Run SAST, DAST, dependency, and container security scans where applicable.

Release phase:
- Obtain required approvals before production deployment.

=================================
16. BUSINESS CONTINUITY POLICY
=================================

Critical business systems must have documented recovery procedures.

Recovery plans must be tested at least once per year.

Backup data must be encrypted and periodically restored to verify reliability.

Critical services must define Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO).

=================================
17. SECURITY AWARENESS TRAINING POLICY
=================================

All employees must complete annual security awareness training.

Employees in engineering, DevOps, IT, and security roles may be required to complete additional technical security training.

Phishing simulations may be conducted periodically to improve employee awareness.

Repeated failure to complete mandatory training may be escalated to management.

=================================
18. MOBILE DEVICE POLICY
=================================

Employees accessing company email or applications from mobile devices must enable screen lock, device encryption, and remote wipe capability.

Jailbroken or rooted devices must not be used to access company systems.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours.

=================================
19. LOGGING AND MONITORING POLICY
=================================

Production systems must generate security-relevant logs for authentication, authorization, administrative actions, and errors.

Logs must be protected against unauthorized modification and retained according to compliance requirements.

Critical alerts must be reviewed by the Security Operations team.

=================================
20. CODE REPOSITORY POLICY
=================================

Source code repositories must enforce branch protection for main or production branches.

Pull requests must receive at least one peer review before merge.

Secrets scanning must be enabled for repositories containing application or infrastructure code.

Repository access must be reviewed periodically and removed when no longer required.
