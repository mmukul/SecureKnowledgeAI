COMPANY POLICY HANDBOOK

1. WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Employees must be available between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time, attend mandatory meetings, maintain a stable internet connection, and follow company security guidelines.
Remote work requests exceeding 3 days per week require HR approval.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Up to 5 unused leave days can be carried forward. Remaining unused leave expires at year-end.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance.

3. INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

All employees must use Multi-Factor Authentication, change passwords every 90 days, report phishing emails immediately, encrypt confidential company data, and lock systems when away from their workstation.
Sharing company credentials is strictly prohibited.
Violation of security policies may result in disciplinary action.

4. ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

Company devices are intended primarily for business use.
Employees must not install unauthorized software, download pirated content, access malicious or illegal websites, or share confidential information externally.
Monitoring may be performed to ensure compliance.

5. INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY

Security incidents must be reported within 30 minutes of discovery.
Critical incidents include data breach, ransomware infection, and unauthorized system access.
High incidents include malware infection and privilege escalation.
Medium incidents include policy violations and unauthorized software installation.
The Security Team will investigate and document all incidents.

6. DEVSECOPS POLICY

All code changes must pass SAST scanning, pass dependency vulnerability scanning, undergo peer review, and have no Critical or High vulnerabilities.
Container images must be scanned before deployment.
Secrets must never be hardcoded in source code.

7. APPLICATION SECURITY POLICY

Development teams must follow secure coding guidelines, conduct threat modeling for critical applications, perform security testing before production releases, remediate Critical vulnerabilities within 24 hours, and remediate High vulnerabilities within 7 days.
OWASP Top 10 risks must be reviewed during development.

8. AI USAGE POLICY

Employees may use approved AI tools for productivity purposes.
Employees must not upload customer PII to public AI systems, upload source code without approval, or share confidential business information with external AI services.
All AI-generated content must be reviewed by a human before publication.

9. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees may claim travel expenses, hotel accommodation, business meals, and conference registrations.
Claims exceeding INR 50,000 require director approval.
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

10. EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING POLICY

New employees must complete Security Awareness Training, Code of Conduct Training, Data Privacy Training, and MFA Enrollment.
Completion is required within the first 14 days of employment.
